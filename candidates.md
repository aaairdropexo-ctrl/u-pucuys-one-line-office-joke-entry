# Candidates — One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint
# Written fresh in this session (first appearance = this file).
# Premises: real office situations (meeting loop, printer, standup,
# reply-all, fake meeting status, jargon, camera off, deadline shuffle).
# Punchline at the end of each line, no explanation after it.

## A — meeting loop
We called a meeting to decide whether we needed a meeting, then booked a follow-up to review what we decided about the meeting.

## B — printer retirement
The office printer has been "warming up" since Tuesday, which is the closest thing this office has to a retirement plan.

## C — standup unblock
In today's standup I said I was blocked, unblocked myself by saying it out loud, and sat back down with nothing done.

## D — reply-all thanks
My inbox has 47 unread emails, 46 of which are replies-all saying "thanks" to the one email that actually mattered.

## E — deadline shuffle
I moved the deadline to Friday, then moved Friday to next week, and now the calendar thinks I am a time traveller.

## F — fake meeting lunch
I set my status to "in a meeting" so I could eat lunch at my desk in peace, and now three people want to join the meeting.

## G — synergise the fruit
My manager told me to circle back and synergise the low-hanging fruit, so I nodded, wrote it down, and still have no idea what fruit.

## H — camera off
I turned my camera off for "connection issues" and have been nodding at a frozen slide for twenty minutes.
